# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy. The light-dependent reactions happen in the thylakoid membrane, where chlorophyll absorbs photons.

The Calvin cycle then fixes carbon dioxide into glucose using ATP and NADPH. RuBisCO is the enzyme that catalyses the first committed step of carbon fixation.
